# PickyMaax single-product storefront

## What I’ll build
- A responsive, single-scroll homepage with sticky navigation, full-height product introduction, trust marquee, product purchase section, three-step usage guide, founder story, reviews, FAQ, email signup, and footer.
- A black-and-gold luxury visual system with warm cream surfaces, editorial serif typography, restrained motion, thin borders, soft shadows, and generous spacing.
- The supplied PickyMaax logo will appear prominently while the wider page avoids the logo’s brighter colors.
- Replaceable, polished placeholder imagery for the topper package and Max, with clear descriptive alternative text.

## Shopping experience
- Quantity controls and Add to cart behavior.
- A keyboard-accessible slide-out cart showing item quantity, remove controls, subtotal, and a clearly labeled placeholder checkout action.
- Accessible expandable product details and FAQ sections.
- In-page navigation for About, How It Works, and FAQ, plus a Shop shortcut.

## Content and finishing
- Use the supplied founder story and pull quote, formatted as the emotional centerpiece.
- Add three clearly marked sample reviews, product detail placeholders, and contact placeholders without presenting invented facts as final.
- Add route-specific title, description, social metadata, and canonical information.
- Check the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, including cart and accordion interactions.

## Technical details
- Implement with the project’s existing React and Tailwind setup.
- Define all black, gold, cream, border, type, shadow, and motion values as reusable design tokens.
- Store the uploaded logo through the project asset system and import its pointer.
- Keep cart state in the page session only; checkout, payments, email delivery, and inventory persistence remain placeholders as requested.
