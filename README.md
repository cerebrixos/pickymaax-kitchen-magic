# PickyMaax Gold Standard

Build a single-product ecommerce landing site for a brand called "PickyMaax" — a savory kibble topper/seasoning made for picky dogs. One product only. No product catalog, no categories.

BRAND FEEL

Luxurious, modern, and minimal — think a high-end skincare or fragrance brand, but for dogs. Lots of white space, generous padding, large type. Warm neutral palette: bone white, soft cream, warm sand, deep charcoal, with a single muted gold or amber accent. Elegant serif for headings (something like Playfair Display or Fraunces) paired with a clean geometric sans for body text (Inter or Satoshi). Subtle fade-and-rise animations on scroll, soft shadows, rounded corners. Nothing loud, no bright primary colors, no cartoon dog clipart. Fully responsive, mobile-first.

PAGES / SECTIONS (single scrolling homepage + a couple of subpages)

1. Sticky transparent navbar that turns solid on scroll: logo wordmark "PICKYMAAX" (letter-spaced, serif), links to About, How It Works, FAQ, and a small "Shop" button.

2. Hero — full viewport. Big serif headline: "For the dog who turns up his nose." Subhead: "A savory topper that makes ordinary kibble irresistible." One primary CTA button ("Get PickyMaax") and a quiet secondary link ("Read Max's story"). Large product image placeholder on the right (or below on mobile) with a soft shadow and subtle floating animation.

3. A slim scrolling marquee strip with short trust phrases: "Made for picky eaters" · "Simple ingredients" · "Just sprinkle and serve" · "Dog-approved by Max."

4. Product section — image on one side, details on the other. Product name, short description, price placeholder ($24), quantity selector, "Add to cart" button, and three short bullet benefits with small line icons. Include a small expandable accordion for Ingredients, Feeding Instructions, and Shipping.

5. "How It Works" — three numbered steps in a clean horizontal row: 1) Sprinkle over kibble. 2) Give it a gentle toss. 3) Watch the bowl disappear.

6. ABOUT US — this is the heart of the site. Give it a warm, editorial feel: a large photo placeholder of a dog on the left, generous serif body copy on the right. Use this story (feel free to format it beautifully with a pull quote):

"PickyMaax started with one very stubborn dog.

Max is my dog, and Max does not eat. He'd sniff his bowl, look up at me like I'd personally insulted him, and walk away. We tried every brand, every flavor, every 'vet recommended' bag on the shelf. Some nights he'd skip dinner entirely, and I'd sit on the kitchen floor next to a full bowl feeling completely useless.

So I started experimenting. I spent weeks mixing and testing different savory blends in our kitchen, trying to find something that would make his same old kibble actually smell like food worth eating. Most of them he ignored. And then one evening I sprinkled a new batch over his bowl, set it down, and he inhaled it. Gone in seconds. He licked the bowl, then looked up for more.

That was it. That was the whole thing.

I'm 15. I didn't set out to start a company — I just wanted my dog to eat. But if PickyMaax can get one more picky dog to finish their dinner, and one more person off the kitchen floor, it's worth sharing."

Include a pull quote styled large and serif: "I just wanted my dog to eat."

End the section with a small handwritten-style signature line and a note: "Made by a 15-year-old and one very picky taste tester."

7. A simple testimonial section with 3 placeholder review cards (name, dog's name, short quote, 5 stars) — keep the cards minimal with thin borders, no heavy shadows.

8. FAQ accordion — 5-6 questions covering: what's in it, how much to use, what if my dog doesn't like it, is it safe for puppies/seniors, shipping times, refunds.

9. Email capture band: "Join the pack" — single input + button, plus one line about early restock access.

10. Footer — logo wordmark, nav links, Instagram/TikTok icons, contact email placeholder, and fine print: "PickyMaax · Made with love (and a lot of failed batches) by a 15-year-old and Max."
